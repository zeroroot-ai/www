## Self-hosted

Run ZeroRoot AI on your own Kubernetes cluster. The platform Helm chart, tenant and platform operators, and execution substrate are available for on-premises deployment under the Elastic License v2 and Apache-2.0.

Self-hosted deployments bypass the hosted billing system entirely — you bring your own infrastructure and licensing agreement.
